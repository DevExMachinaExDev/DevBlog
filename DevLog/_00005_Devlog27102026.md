## Key Takeaways

- Revisiting questions is good for exposing small gaps in understanding.
- Talking to AI as an interviewer is useful for practice but slow.
- The interviewer process might be stronger for practicing defense of design more than anything else.
- Tracking information in sliding windows is harder than the problem itself.
- Bitmasks are only good for tracking existence and not position.
- Readability and maintainability are often worth prioritising over forcing a more space efficient solution.
- Different implementations can have the same time complexity while varying significantly in clarity and ease of debugging.
