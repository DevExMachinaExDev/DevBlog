Another day in the 5 week leetcode training protocol. Today we had 4 questions.

- product of array except self. (revisit)
- 3sum
- Move zeroes
- best time to buy and sell stock

The recurring theme of the day was definitely different variations of two pointer problems or i guess more accurately multiple pointers if you cound the 3 sums third variable. A two pointer approach essentially has you store the positions in array of multiple things at once with their own separate move logic depending on the constraints of the problem and each of these problems moved pointers in their own way.

## product of array except self

Not a two pointer problem but runs through an array backwards and forwards to fill in both sets of data. A reminder that we don't always have to do everything in one go.

## 3sum
An expansion on earlier knowledge with the 2sum2 solution being part of this one and sorting the string making it much easier. The lesson being perhaps that in O(N^2) or higher problems we should probably not be afraid of sorting our data to make lookups and comparisons faster and more structured.

## move zeroes

A reminder that a two pointer can be that the "i" runs ahead and the other pointer stays behind to do the work.

## best time to buy and sell stock

A good reminder that sometimes we just want to store the values and update rather than keep pointers for positions and over complicate things.

## Key takeaways

- Two pointer problems come in a lot of varieties.

- If an O(N²) problem starts becoming a nightmare of maps, sets, duplicate tracking and special cases then there is a decent chance I am approaching it from the wrong angle.

- Sorting is not a crime. Using it to simplify in higher complexity problems should be considered.

- The "Framing effect" means that a questions wording can effect how you approach it and it needs to be watched closely.
